# Account Deletion Draft - Legal Review Required

Users should be able to request account deletion in-app or through `dev.hamdhytech@gmail.com`. Identity and authority must be verified before action. Removing a parent account should revoke parent-student links and notification tokens. Removing staff access should disable authentication and memberships immediately.

Deletion of an account may not erase attendance or audit records that the institute must retain. Such records should be minimized, access-restricted, and handled under the retention policy. Define timelines, appeals, backups, and legal holds before production.
