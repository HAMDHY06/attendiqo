# Privacy Policy Draft - Legal Review Required

This draft describes the planned Attendiqo and Attendiqo Connect services from HamdhyTech. It is not production legal text and must be reviewed before publication.

The planned system may collect student name, student number, institute and class details, attendance entry and departure times, parent or guardian name and mobile number, parent account information, device notification tokens, and notification/SMS delivery logs. Student photographs are **not collected or stored**.

Data is intended only for attendance administration, approved parent access, operational notices, security, auditing, and optional consent-based SMS. Access will be limited by role, institute, assigned class, and approved parent-student link. Data will not be exposed through QR codes; QR payloads use opaque tokens.

Users may request access, correction, or deletion subject to legal, safeguarding, and institute record obligations. Contact `dev.hamdhytech@gmail.com`. Retention periods, subprocessors, lawful bases, cross-border processing, incident procedures, and jurisdiction-specific rights require legal review.
